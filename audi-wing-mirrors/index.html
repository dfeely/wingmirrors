Audi wing mirrors

Advice for choosing and replacing wing mirrors on Audi cars sold in Ireland.

About Audi wing mirrors

Audi mirrors are among the more complex wing mirrors, with options such as power folding, memory, auto-dimming, lane assist and puddle lights. That means two A4s of the same year can need quite different parts. Before ordering, check the options on your car, and if possible the part number on the back of the old unit.

What to check before ordering

  • Which side: driver's (right) or passenger (left).
  • Glass only, cap only or complete unit.
  • Electric or manual adjustment, and whether it is heated.
  • Folding, indicator, blind spot or auto-dimming features.
  • Year of manufacture, since parts change at facelifts.

Audi wing mirror FAQs

Why does my Audi mirror have so many wires?

Features such as heating, folding, dimming and side assist each use additional connections, so the plug varies by specification.

Can I replace just the glass on an Audi auto-dimming mirror?

Yes, but you need the correct glass type. Auto-dimming glass is not interchangeable with standard heated glass.

Which side is the driver's side Audi wing mirror in Ireland?

The right-hand (offside) mirror. The left-hand (nearside) mirror is the passenger side.
