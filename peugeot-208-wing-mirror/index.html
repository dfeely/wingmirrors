Peugeot 208 wing mirror: generations, types and replacement

Which Peugeot 208 mirror you need, how generations differ and what to check before you order.

Peugeot 208 generations and mirror differences

GenerationYearsMirror notes
First generation2012-2019The earlier 208. A 2015 facelift sits within this range.
Second generation2019 onwardsA new design, including an electric version, with different mirrors. Not interchangeable with the first generation.

Year ranges are approximate and based on European sales. Check your registration and handbook for your car.

Which Peugeot 208 mirror do you have?

Features depend on year, market and trim. Cars like yours may come with:

  • Manual or electric adjustment on first-generation cars
  • Heated glass on some trims
  • Folding on some trims
  • Indicator repeater in the housing on many cars
  • Blind spot monitoring on some newer cars

Test your own car: does the mirror adjust electrically, fold, show an indicator, or clear mist with the rear screen heater on? Match those features when ordering.

Common Peugeot 208 wing mirror problems

  • Loose or detached glass
  • Folding mechanism faults
  • Damaged caps

Tip for your 208

Peugeot glass can come away from the backing plate. If the plate is intact, re-fitting new glass is a simple fix.

Glass, cap or full unit?

If only the glass is cracked, glass alone is usually enough. A broken housing, a dead motor or a failed indicator usually means a full unit. See glass vs full unit and replacing a wing mirror.

Peugeot 208 wing mirror FAQs

Does the electric e-208 have a different mirror?

It shares its look with the second-generation 208, but check your exact car and trim before ordering.

My 208 mirror glass has come loose, what now?

Check whether the backing plate is intact. If so, new glass clips on. If the plate is broken, you may need a full unit.

Which side is the driver's side Peugeot 208 wing mirror in Ireland?

The right-hand (offside) mirror. The left-hand (nearside) mirror is the passenger side.
