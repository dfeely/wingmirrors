Advice for choosing and replacing wing mirrors on Land Rover cars sold in Ireland.

About Land Rover wing mirrors

Land Rover mirrors are some of the most feature-rich on the road. Depending on model and year, they can include power folding, heated glass, auto-dimming, blind spot monitoring, puddle lights and surround-view cameras. That makes the exact specification important, and a wrong guess can be an expensive mistake.

What to check before ordering

  • Which side: driver's (right) or passenger (left).
  • Glass only, cap only or complete unit.
  • Electric or manual adjustment, and whether it is heated.
  • Folding, indicator, blind spot or auto-dimming features.
  • Year of manufacture, since parts change at facelifts.

Land Rover wing mirror FAQs

Why are Land Rover mirrors so complicated to order?

Features such as folding, cameras, puddle lights and blind spot monitoring change the unit's wiring and fittings. Two cars of the same model and year can need different mirrors.

Is it worth repairing a Land Rover mirror or replacing the whole unit?

If only the glass is damaged, glass alone is much cheaper. If the housing, motor or electronics are faulty, a full unit is usually the practical fix.

Which side is the driver's side Land Rover wing mirror in Ireland?

The right-hand (offside) mirror. The left-hand (nearside) mirror is the passenger side.
