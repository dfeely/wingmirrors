Land Rover Range Rover Evoque wing mirror: generations, types and replacement

Which Land Rover Range Rover Evoque mirror you need, how generations differ and what to check before you order.

Land Rover Range Rover Evoque generations and mirror differences

GenerationYearsMirror notes
First generation2011-2019The original Evoque, with updates during its life. Options vary a lot by trim.
Second generation2019 onwardsNew design with a different mirror and more driver-assist features. Not interchangeable with the first generation.

Year ranges are approximate and based on European sales. Check your registration and handbook for your car.

Which Land Rover Range Rover Evoque mirror do you have?

Features depend on year, market and trim. Cars like yours may come with:

  • Electric adjustment and heated glass on most cars
  • Power folding on many trims
  • Auto-dimming glass on some trims
  • Puddle lamp on some cars
  • Blind spot monitoring on some trims
  • Camera equipment on some higher trims

Test your own car: does the mirror adjust electrically, fold, show an indicator, or clear mist with the rear screen heater on? Match those features when ordering.

Common Land Rover Range Rover Evoque wing mirror problems

  • Fold motor or fold mechanism faults
  • Cracked glass
  • Damage to the housing in tight parking

Tip for your Range Rover Evoque

Take a photo of the back of your old mirror and the plug. It helps confirm the specification before you order.

Glass, cap or full unit?

If only the glass is cracked, glass alone is usually enough. A broken housing, a dead motor or a failed indicator usually means a full unit. See glass vs full unit and replacing a wing mirror.

Land Rover Range Rover Evoque wing mirror FAQs

Are Evoque mirrors different between 2012 and 2018?

Updates during the first generation can affect details. Always confirm your exact year and specification.

What is the light under my Evoque mirror?

It is usually a puddle lamp that lights the ground by the door. If you have one, order a mirror that includes it.

Which side is the driver's side Land Rover Range Rover Evoque wing mirror in Ireland?

The right-hand (offside) mirror. The left-hand (nearside) mirror is the passenger side.

Need help finding the right mirror?

Send us your registration number, which side (driver or passenger) and a photo of the back of the mirror, and we will point you to the correct part.
