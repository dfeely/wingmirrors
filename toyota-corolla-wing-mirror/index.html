Toyota Corolla wing mirror: generations, types and replacement

Which Toyota Corolla mirror you need, how generations differ and what to check before you order.

Which Toyota Corolla mirror do you have?

Features depend on year, market and trim. Cars like yours may come with:

  • Manual or electric adjustment depending on age and trim
  • Heated glass on many mid and upper trims
  • Folding on some trims
  • Indicator repeater on many cars
  • Blind spot monitoring on some newer versions, with a warning light in the mirror

Test your own car: does the mirror adjust electrically, fold, show an indicator, or clear mist with the rear screen heater on? Match those features when ordering.

Common Toyota Corolla wing mirror problems

  • Broken caps and housings after kerb or gate-post contact
  • Cracked glass
  • Loose or rattling glass on older cars

Tip for your Corolla

If your car is a pre-2019 hatchback, it is probably an Auris. Use that name to compare parts, then confirm the year.

Glass, cap or full unit?

If only the glass is cracked, glass alone is usually enough. A broken housing, a dead motor or a failed indicator usually means a full unit. See glass vs full unit and replacing a wing mirror.

Toyota Corolla wing mirror FAQs

Is a Corolla mirror the same as an Auris mirror?

No. They are different cars and different generations, so the parts differ. Match your exact model name and year.

Does my Corolla have blind spot monitoring?

Only some newer trims do. If there is a small warning light in the mirror housing or glass, you will need a mirror that supports it.

Which side is the driver's side Toyota Corolla wing mirror in Ireland?

The right-hand (offside) mirror. The left-hand (nearside) mirror is the passenger side.
