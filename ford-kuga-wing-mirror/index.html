Ford Kuga wing mirror: generations, types and replacement

Which Ford Kuga mirror you need, how generations differ and what to check before you order.

Ford Kuga generations and mirror differences

GenerationYearsMirror notes
First generation2008-2012Older cars with simpler mirror options.
Second generation2012-2019A facelift in 2016 sits within this range. Heated and folding options are common on higher trims.
Third generation2019 onwardsIncludes hybrid and plug-in versions. A different design from earlier cars.

Year ranges are approximate and based on European sales. Check your registration and handbook for your car.

Which Ford Kuga mirror do you have?

Features depend on year, market and trim. Cars like yours may come with:

  • Electric adjustment on most cars
  • Heated glass on many trims
  • Power folding on some trims
  • Indicator repeater in the housing
  • Blind spot warning (BLIS) on some cars, with a light in the mirror
  • Puddle light on some higher trims

Test your own car: does the mirror adjust electrically, fold, show an indicator, or clear mist with the rear screen heater on? Match those features when ordering.

Common Ford Kuga wing mirror problems

  • Indicator or fold faults
  • Cracked glass or housing damage
  • Cap damage from tight parking

Tip for your Kuga

If you see a light on the glass or housing when you pass another vehicle, your Kuga likely has blind spot detection.

Glass, cap or full unit?

If only the glass is cracked, glass alone is usually enough. A broken housing, a dead motor or a failed indicator usually means a full unit. See glass vs full unit and replacing a wing mirror. For all your options, see wing mirror replacement.

Ford Kuga wing mirror FAQs

Is the hybrid Kuga mirror different?

The body and mirror design are generally shared across a generation, but trim and options differ. Match your features.

Does a second-generation Kuga mirror fit the third?

No. They are different generations.

Which side is the driver's side Ford Kuga wing mirror in Ireland?

The right-hand (offside) mirror. The left-hand (nearside) mirror is the passenger side.
