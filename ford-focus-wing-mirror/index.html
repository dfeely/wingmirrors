Ford Focus wing mirror: generations, types and replacement

Which Ford Focus mirror you need, how generations differ and what to check before you order.

Ford Focus generations and mirror differences

GenerationYearsMirror notes
Mk22004-2011A facelift in 2008 changed styling. Older cars often have simpler mirrors.
Mk32011-2018A 2014 facelift sits within this range. Many have electric heated mirrors, and some have folding.
Mk42018-2025Latest generation. More driver-assist features, some of which affect the mirror.

Year ranges are approximate and based on European sales. Check your registration and handbook for your car.

Which Ford Focus mirror do you have?

Features depend on year, market and trim. Cars like yours may come with:

  • Manual or cable adjustment on some older cars
  • Electric adjust and heated glass on many trims
  • Power folding on some trims
  • Indicator repeater in the housing or cap
  • Blind spot warning (BLIS) on some cars, with a light in the mirror

Test your own car: does the mirror adjust electrically, fold, show an indicator, or clear mist with the rear screen heater on? Match those features when ordering.

Common Ford Focus wing mirror problems

  • Broken glass or housing after car park knocks
  • Indicator or fold function stopping
  • Worn adjustment motors on older cars

Tip for your Focus

If you have a warning light near the mirror glass, your Focus may have blind spot detection. Replace like for like.

Glass, cap or full unit?

If only the glass is cracked, glass alone is usually enough. A broken housing, a dead motor or a failed indicator usually means a full unit. See glass vs full unit and replacing a wing mirror.

Ford Focus wing mirror FAQs

Will a Mk3 Focus mirror fit a Mk4?

No. They are different generations and different parts. Order for your generation and year.

What does the light in my Focus mirror mean?

On cars with blind spot detection it warns you of a vehicle alongside. If yours has it, use a mirror that includes that function.

Which side is the driver's side Ford Focus wing mirror in Ireland?

The right-hand (offside) mirror. The left-hand (nearside) mirror is the passenger side.
