Kia Picanto wing mirror: generations, types and replacement

Which Kia Picanto mirror you need, how generations differ and what to check before you order.

Kia Picanto generations and mirror differences

GenerationYearsMirror notes
First generation2004-2011Older cars with simple mirrors.
Second generation2011-2017Updated styling. Manual or electric depending on trim.
Third generation2017 onwardsNewer design with a 2020 update. Higher trims add folding and indicators.

Year ranges are approximate and based on European sales. Check your registration and handbook for your car.

Which Kia Picanto mirror do you have?

Features depend on year, market and trim. Cars like yours may come with:

  • Manual adjustment on base trims
  • Electric adjustment on higher trims
  • Heated glass on some trims
  • Indicator repeater on some versions
  • Folding on some trims

Test your own car: does the mirror adjust electrically, fold, show an indicator, or clear mist with the rear screen heater on? Match those features when ordering.

Common Kia Picanto wing mirror problems

  • Cracked glass from parking knocks
  • Broken caps
  • Loose glass on older cars

Tip for your Picanto

Picanto repairs are often glass-only. Check the housing first.

Glass, cap or full unit?

If only the glass is cracked, glass alone is usually enough. A broken housing, a dead motor or a failed indicator usually means a full unit. See glass vs full unit and replacing a wing mirror.

Kia Picanto wing mirror FAQs

Will a second-generation Picanto mirror fit a third?

No. They are different designs.

Is my Picanto mirror manual or electric?

If there is a switch inside the car for the mirrors, it is electric. If you push the glass or use a lever, it is manual.

Which side is the driver's side Kia Picanto wing mirror in Ireland?

The right-hand (offside) mirror. The left-hand (nearside) mirror is the passenger side.
