Advice for choosing and replacing wing mirrors on Peugeot cars sold in Ireland.

About Peugeot wing mirrors

Peugeot's 207, 208 and 308 are familiar sights in Ireland, along with the Partner van. A frequent complaint is a mirror glass coming loose or the folding mechanism failing. Many mirrors have the indicator integrated into the cap, which affects what you need to order.

What to check before ordering

  • Which side: driver's (right) or passenger (left).
  • Glass only, cap only or complete unit.
  • Electric or manual adjustment, and whether it is heated.
  • Folding, indicator, blind spot or auto-dimming features.
  • Year of manufacture, since parts change at facelifts.

Peugeot wing mirror FAQs

My Peugeot mirror glass has fallen off, can I fix it?

Often the glass can be re-clipped or replaced. If the backing plate is broken, you may need new glass or a full unit.

Do Peugeot 208 mirrors differ by year?

Yes. The first and second generation 208 look similar but are different parts.

Which side is the driver's side Peugeot wing mirror in Ireland?

The right-hand (offside) mirror. The left-hand (nearside) mirror is the passenger side.

Need help finding the right mirror?
