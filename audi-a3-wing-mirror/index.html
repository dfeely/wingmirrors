Audi A3 wing mirror: generations, types and replacement

Which Audi A3 mirror you need, how generations differ and what to check before you order.

Audi A3 generations and mirror differences

GenerationYearsMirror notes
8P2003-2012A facelift around 2008 sits in this range. Options vary.
8V2012-2020A facelift around 2016. Power folding and side assist available on some trims.
8Y2020 onwardsLatest generation with a different design.

Year ranges are approximate and based on European sales. Check your registration and handbook for your car.

Which Audi A3 mirror do you have?

Features depend on year, market and trim. Cars like yours may come with:

  • Electric adjustment and heated glass on most cars
  • Power folding on some trims
  • Auto-dimming glass on some trims
  • Indicator repeater in the housing on many cars
  • Side assist (blind spot) on some cars
  • Puddle light on some higher trims

Test your own car: does the mirror adjust electrically, fold, show an indicator, or clear mist with the rear screen heater on? Match those features when ordering.

Common Audi A3 wing mirror problems

  • Folding motor faults
  • Heated glass failure
  • Glass detaching
  • Indicator faults

Tip for your A3

Two A3s of the same year can need different mirrors. Check folding, dimming, side assist and any puddle light.

Glass, cap or full unit?

If only the glass is cracked, glass alone is usually enough. A broken housing, a dead motor or a failed indicator usually means a full unit. See glass vs full unit and replacing a wing mirror. For all your options, see wing mirror replacement.

Audi A3 wing mirror FAQs

Can I replace just the glass on an Audi A3?

Yes, if the housing and electrics are fine. Match the glass type, such as heated or auto-dimming.

Does an 8P A3 mirror fit an 8V?

No. They are different generations.

Which side is the driver's side Audi A3 wing mirror in Ireland?

The right-hand (offside) mirror. The left-hand (nearside) mirror is the passenger side.
