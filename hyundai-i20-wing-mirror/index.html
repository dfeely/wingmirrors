Hyundai i20 wing mirror: generations, types and replacement

Which Hyundai i20 mirror you need, how generations differ and what to check before you order.

Hyundai i20 generations and mirror differences

GenerationYearsMirror notes
First generation (PB)2008-2014Older cars with simpler mirrors.
Second generation (GB)2014-2020A facelift sits within this range. Electric and heated options on higher trims.
Third generation (BC3)2020 onwardsNewer design with more equipment. Not interchangeable with earlier cars.

Year ranges are approximate and based on European sales. Check your registration and handbook for your car.

Which Hyundai i20 mirror do you have?

Features depend on year, market and trim. Cars like yours may come with:

  • Manual or electric adjustment depending on trim
  • Heated glass on some trims
  • Folding on some trims
  • Indicator repeater on many cars
  • Blind spot warning on some newer cars

Test your own car: does the mirror adjust electrically, fold, show an indicator, or clear mist with the rear screen heater on? Match those features when ordering.

Common Hyundai i20 wing mirror problems

  • Cracked glass from parking knocks
  • Indicator faults on built-in repeaters
  • Cap damage

Tip for your i20

Many i20 repairs are glass-only. Check the housing first before ordering a full unit.

Glass, cap or full unit?

If only the glass is cracked, glass alone is usually enough. A broken housing, a dead motor or a failed indicator usually means a full unit. See glass vs full unit and replacing a wing mirror. For all your options, see wing mirror replacement.

Hyundai i20 wing mirror FAQs

Does a GB i20 mirror fit a BC3?

No. They are different generations with different designs.

My i20 mirror indicator has stopped, do I need a whole mirror?

On many cars the indicator is part of the housing, so a full unit may be the fix. Check whether yours can be repaired.

Which side is the driver's side Hyundai i20 wing mirror in Ireland?

The right-hand (offside) mirror. The left-hand (nearside) mirror is the passenger side.
