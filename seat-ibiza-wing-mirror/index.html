SEAT Ibiza wing mirror: generations, types and replacement

Which SEAT Ibiza mirror you need, how generations differ and what to check before you order.

SEAT Ibiza generations and mirror differences

GenerationYearsMirror notes
6L2002-2008Older cars with simple mirrors.
6J2008-2017The most common on used lists. A facelift came around 2012.
6F2017 onwardsNewer design. Not interchangeable with earlier cars.

Year ranges are approximate and based on European sales. Check your registration and handbook for your car.

Which SEAT Ibiza mirror do you have?

Features depend on year, market and trim. Cars like yours may come with:

  • Manual or electric adjustment depending on trim
  • Heated glass on some trims
  • Folding on some trims
  • Indicator repeater on later cars

Test your own car: does the mirror adjust electrically, fold, show an indicator, or clear mist with the rear screen heater on? Match those features when ordering.

Common SEAT Ibiza wing mirror problems

  • Cracked glass
  • Cap damage
  • Loose glass on older cars

Tip for your Ibiza

Many Ibiza repairs are glass or cap only. Check what is actually damaged first.

Glass, cap or full unit?

If only the glass is cracked, glass alone is usually enough. A broken housing, a dead motor or a failed indicator usually means a full unit. See glass vs full unit and replacing a wing mirror. For all your options, see wing mirror replacement.

SEAT Ibiza wing mirror FAQs

Is an Ibiza mirror the same as a Polo mirror?

No. The cars share some engineering, but the mirrors are different parts.

Does a 6J Ibiza mirror fit a 6F?

No. They are different generations.

Which side is the driver's side SEAT Ibiza wing mirror in Ireland?

The right-hand (offside) mirror. The left-hand (nearside) mirror is the passenger side.

Need help finding the right mirror?

Send us your registration number, which side (driver or passenger) and a photo of the back of the mirror, and we will point you to the correct part.
