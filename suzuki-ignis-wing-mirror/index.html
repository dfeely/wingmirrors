Which Suzuki Ignis mirror do you have?

Features depend on year, market and trim. Cars like yours may come with:

  • Electric adjustment on most trims
  • Heated glass on some trims
  • Indicator repeater on some versions
  • Contrasting or body-coloured caps depending on trim

Test your own car: does the mirror adjust electrically, fold, show an indicator, or clear mist with the rear screen heater on? Match those features when ordering.

Common Suzuki Ignis wing mirror problems

  • Scuffed caps
  • Cracked glass
  • Housing damage from parking

Tip for your Ignis

The Ignis has distinctive styling, so check that the mirror matches your trim and any contrasting colour caps.

Glass, cap or full unit?

If only the glass is cracked, glass alone is usually enough. A broken housing, a dead motor or a failed indicator usually means a full unit. See glass vs full unit and replacing a wing mirror.

Suzuki Ignis wing mirror FAQs

Is an Ignis mirror the same as a Swift mirror?

No. They are different cars with different mirror designs.

Can I change just the cap on my Ignis?

Often yes. Match the side and colour, and check whether your car has a contrasting cap.

Which side is the driver's side Suzuki Ignis wing mirror in Ireland?

The right-hand (offside) mirror. The left-hand (nearside) mirror is the passenger side.
