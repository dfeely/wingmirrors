Dacia Sandero wing mirror: generations, types and replacement

Which Dacia Sandero mirror you need, how generations differ and what to check before you order.

Dacia Sandero generations and mirror differences

GenerationYearsMirror notes
First generation2008-2012Early cars with simple mirrors. Manual adjustment is common.
Second generation2012-2020The generation most often seen on used lists. Stepway versions have slightly different styling.
Third generation2020 onwardsNew design with a different mirror. Not interchangeable with the earlier cars.

Year ranges are approximate and based on European sales. Check your registration and handbook for your car.

Which Dacia Sandero mirror do you have?

Features depend on year, market and trim. Cars like yours may come with:

  • Manual adjustment on base trims
  • Electric adjustment on higher trims
  • Heated glass on some trims
  • Body-coloured or black caps depending on trim
  • Indicator repeater on some newer cars

Test your own car: does the mirror adjust electrically, fold, show an indicator, or clear mist with the rear screen heater on? Match those features when ordering.

Common Dacia Sandero wing mirror problems

  • Scuffed or broken caps
  • Cracked glass
  • Adjustment faults on older manual or cable mirrors

Tip for your Sandero

Many Sandero repairs are glass or cap only. Check the housing first before paying for a complete unit.

Glass, cap or full unit?

If only the glass is cracked, glass alone is usually enough. A broken housing, a dead motor or a failed indicator usually means a full unit. See glass vs full unit and replacing a wing mirror. For all your options, see wing mirror replacement.

Dacia Sandero wing mirror FAQs

Is a Sandero Stepway mirror different from a standard Sandero?

Within the same generation they are often similar, but styling and trim can differ. Match your exact car and year.

How do I tell my Sandero's generation?

Use the registration year as a guide. The generations changed around 2012 and 2020, but check your handbook.

Which side is the driver's side Dacia Sandero wing mirror in Ireland?

The right-hand (offside) mirror. The left-hand (nearside) mirror is the passenger side.

Need help finding the right mirror?

Send us your registration number, which side (driver or passenger) and a photo of the back of the mirror, and we will point you to the correct part.
