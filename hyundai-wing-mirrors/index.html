Hyundai wing mirrors

Advice for choosing and replacing wing mirrors on Hyundai cars sold in Ireland.

About Hyundai wing mirrors

Hyundai has a strong presence in Ireland, especially the i20, i30 and Tucson. Many models have a separate indicator repeater in the mirror housing, and this is a common failure point. When ordering, confirm whether yours has an integrated indicator and heated glass.

What to check before ordering

  • Which side: driver's (right) or passenger (left).
  • Glass only, cap only or complete unit.
  • Electric or manual adjustment, and whether it is heated.
  • Folding, indicator, blind spot or auto-dimming features.
  • Year of manufacture, since parts change at facelifts.

Hyundai wing mirror FAQs

My Hyundai mirror indicator has stopped, do I need a new mirror?

Sometimes only the indicator module is faulty, but on many models it is part of the housing, so a complete unit may be the practical fix.

Is the left or right mirror the driver's side on my Hyundai?

In an Irish right-hand-drive car the driver's side is the right (offside) mirror and the passenger side is the left (nearside).

Which side is the driver's side Hyundai wing mirror in Ireland?

The right-hand (offside) mirror. The left-hand (nearside) mirror is the passenger side.
