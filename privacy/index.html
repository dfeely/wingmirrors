Privacy policy

wingmirrors.ie is operated by Feely Consultants, 7 Grangebrook Avenue, Dublin 16, Ireland. This page explains what information we handle and why. Last updated: November 2026.

Information you send us

If you contact us by email or phone, for example with your car registration or a photo, we use your details only to reply to your enquiry. We do not sell your personal information, and we keep enquiries only as long as we need them to deal with your request.

Analytics and cookies

This site uses Google Tag Manager, which may load analytics tools that use cookies or similar technologies to show us how the site is used, such as which pages are visited and what type of device is used. You can control or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process visitors' IP addresses in order to serve the site.

Your rights

Under data protection law you can ask us for access to, correction of or deletion of personal information we hold about you. Contact us at sales@wingmirrors.ie. You can also complain to the Irish Data Protection Commission.
