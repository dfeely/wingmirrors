Which Tesla Model 3 mirror you need, how generations differ and what to check before you order.

Which Tesla Model 3 mirror do you have?

Features depend on year, market and trim. Cars like yours may come with:

  • Electric adjustment on all cars
  • Heated glass on most cars
  • Power folding on most cars
  • Auto-dimming glass on some
  • Indicator and sensor equipment, which varies by version

Test your own car: does the mirror adjust electrically, fold, show an indicator, or clear mist with the rear screen heater on? Match those features when ordering.

Common Tesla Model 3 wing mirror problems

  • Cracked glass
  • Fold motor faults
  • Housing damage from parking knocks

Tip for your Model 3

Take a photo of the back of your old mirror and the plug. Specifications vary, so matching like for like is important.

Glass, cap or full unit?

If only the glass is cracked, glass alone is usually enough. A broken housing, a dead motor or a failed indicator usually means a full unit. See glass vs full unit and replacing a wing mirror. For all your options, see wing mirror replacement.

Tesla Model 3 wing mirror FAQs

Does a pre-refresh Model 3 mirror fit the new one?

Do not assume so. Updates may change the housing and fittings, so check your exact car.

Do Model 3 mirrors have a camera in them?

Not typically in the mirror housing, but specifications vary. Check your own car.

Which side is the driver's side Tesla Model 3 wing mirror in Ireland?

The right-hand (offside) mirror. The left-hand (nearside) mirror is the passenger side.

Need help finding the right mirror?

Send us your registration number, which side (driver or passenger) and a photo of the back of the mirror, and we will point you to the correct part.
