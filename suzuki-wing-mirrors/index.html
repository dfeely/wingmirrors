Suzuki wing mirrors

Advice for choosing and replacing wing mirrors on Suzuki cars sold in Ireland.

About Suzuki wing mirrors

Suzuki's Swift, Vitara and Ignis are common small cars on Irish roads, many of them kept for years by loyal owners. Simpler cars mean mirror replacements are often glass or cap jobs, but specifications still change between generations and trims, so the year and equipment level matter.

What to check before ordering

  • Which side: driver's (right) or passenger (left).
  • Glass only, cap only or complete unit.
  • Electric or manual adjustment, and whether it is heated.
  • Folding, indicator, blind spot or auto-dimming features.
  • Year of manufacture, since parts change at facelifts.

Suzuki wing mirror FAQs

How do I know which generation of Suzuki Swift I have?

The registration year is the quickest guide. The Swift changed generation around 2010, 2017 and 2024, but check your handbook for your exact car.

Does my Suzuki mirror have an indicator in it?

Some trims do. If there is an indicator lens in the mirror housing, match that when ordering.

Which side is the driver's side Suzuki wing mirror in Ireland?

The right-hand (offside) mirror. The left-hand (nearside) mirror is the passenger side.

Need help finding the right mirror?

Send us your registration number, which side (driver or passenger) and a photo of the back of the mirror, and we will point you to the correct part.
