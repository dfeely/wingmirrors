Hyundai i30 wing mirror: generations, types and replacement

Which Hyundai i30 mirror you need, how generations differ and what to check before you order.

Hyundai i30 generations and mirror differences

GenerationYearsMirror notes
FD2007-2011First generation. Mirror options are simpler on many cars.
GD2011-2017The generation most often seen on used lists. A facelift sits in this range.
PD2017 onwardsNewer generation with a different design and more driver-assist options.

Year ranges are approximate and based on European sales. Check your registration and handbook for your car.

Which Hyundai i30 mirror do you have?

Features depend on year, market and trim. Cars like yours may come with:

  • Manual or electric adjustment depending on year
  • Heated glass on many mid and upper trims
  • Folding on some cars
  • Indicator repeater on some versions
  • Blind spot detection on some newer trims

Test your own car: does the mirror adjust electrically, fold, show an indicator, or clear mist with the rear screen heater on? Match those features when ordering.

Common Hyundai i30 wing mirror problems

  • Built-in indicator failing on some cars
  • Glass cracking or coming loose
  • Adjustment motor failing on older cars

Tip for your i30

A quick test of the electric adjuster, fold button and indicator tells you which features to match before ordering.

Glass, cap or full unit?

If only the glass is cracked, glass alone is usually enough. A broken housing, a dead motor or a failed indicator usually means a full unit. See glass vs full unit and replacing a wing mirror.

Hyundai i30 wing mirror FAQs

Can I fit a GD i30 mirror to a PD car?

No. They are different generations with different fittings.

My i30 mirror indicator doesn't work, do I need a new mirror?

Sometimes it is a bulb or LED module, but on many cars the repeater is part of the housing, so a full unit may be the fix.

Which side is the driver's side Hyundai i30 wing mirror in Ireland?

The right-hand (offside) mirror. The left-hand (nearside) mirror is the passenger side.
