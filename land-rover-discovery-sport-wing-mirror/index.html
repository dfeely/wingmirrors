Which Land Rover Discovery Sport mirror do you have?

Features depend on year, market and trim. Cars like yours may come with:

  • Electric adjustment and heated glass on most cars
  • Power folding on many trims
  • Auto-dimming glass on some trims
  • Puddle light under the mirror on some cars
  • Blind spot monitoring on some trims
  • Camera equipment on some higher trims

Test your own car: does the mirror adjust electrically, fold, show an indicator, or clear mist with the rear screen heater on? Match those features when ordering.

Common Land Rover Discovery Sport wing mirror problems

  • Fold motor not working
  • Cracked glass or damaged housing
  • Faults with puddle light or camera units on higher-spec mirrors

Tip for your Discovery Sport

Look underneath the mirror for a lens or camera. If you have one, your mirror needs to include that equipment.

Glass, cap or full unit?

If only the glass is cracked, glass alone is usually enough. A broken housing, a dead motor or a failed indicator usually means a full unit. See glass vs full unit and replacing a wing mirror. For all your options, see wing mirror replacement.

Land Rover Discovery Sport wing mirror FAQs

Does my Discovery Sport have a camera in the mirror?

Some higher trims do. A small lens on the underside of the housing is a clue.

Can I fit a basic mirror to a high-spec Discovery Sport?

A basic unit may not support folding, cameras or blind spot functions, so match your original specification.

Which side is the driver's side Land Rover Discovery Sport wing mirror in Ireland?

The right-hand (offside) mirror. The left-hand (nearside) mirror is the passenger side.
