Kia wing mirrors

Advice for choosing and replacing wing mirrors on Kia cars sold in Ireland.

About Kia wing mirrors

Kia's Picanto, Ceed and Sportage are very common here, many of them on a long warranty. If your car is still covered, check the terms before ordering aftermarket parts. Higher trims commonly have electric folding and an indicator in the mirror cap.

What to check before ordering

  • Which side: driver's (right) or passenger (left).
  • Glass only, cap only or complete unit.
  • Electric or manual adjustment, and whether it is heated.
  • Folding, indicator, blind spot or auto-dimming features.
  • Year of manufacture, since parts change at facelifts.

Kia wing mirror FAQs

Will an aftermarket mirror affect my Kia warranty?

Warranty terms vary, and damage from a collision is typically not covered anyway. Check with your dealer if you are unsure.

Do Kia mirrors fold electrically?

Many mid and upper trims do. Check whether your plug has the extra pins needed for a folding motor.

Which side is the driver's side Kia wing mirror in Ireland?

The right-hand (offside) mirror. The left-hand (nearside) mirror is the passenger side.

Need help finding the right mirror?

Send us your registration number, which side (driver or passenger) and a photo of the back of the mirror, and we will point you to the correct part.
