Volkswagen Tiguan wing mirror: generations, types and replacement

Which Volkswagen Tiguan mirror you need, how generations differ and what to check before you order.

Volkswagen Tiguan generations and mirror differences

GenerationYearsMirror notes
First generation2007-2016A facelift around 2011 sits in this range. Options vary by trim.
Second generation2016-2023Includes the longer Allspace version. A facelift came around 2020.
Third generation2023 onwardsLatest design with a different mirror. Not interchangeable with earlier cars.

Year ranges are approximate and based on European sales. Check your registration and handbook for your car.

Which Volkswagen Tiguan mirror do you have?

Features depend on year, market and trim. Cars like yours may come with:

  • Electric adjustment and heated glass on most cars
  • Power folding on many trims
  • Auto-dimming glass on some trims
  • Blind spot warning on some cars
  • Camera or surround view equipment on some higher trims
  • Indicator repeater in the mirror

Test your own car: does the mirror adjust electrically, fold, show an indicator, or clear mist with the rear screen heater on? Match those features when ordering.

Common Volkswagen Tiguan wing mirror problems

  • Folding motor faults
  • Cracked glass
  • Wiring issues on cars with many options

Tip for your Tiguan

Look under your mirror for a small lens. If there is one, your Tiguan has a camera function and needs a matching mirror.

Glass, cap or full unit?

If only the glass is cracked, glass alone is usually enough. A broken housing, a dead motor or a failed indicator usually means a full unit. See glass vs full unit and replacing a wing mirror. For all your options, see wing mirror replacement.

Volkswagen Tiguan wing mirror FAQs

Is the Tiguan Allspace mirror the same as the standard Tiguan?

Within the same generation they are often similar, but confirm with your year and options.

Does my Tiguan mirror contain a camera?

Some trims do. A small lens on the underside of the housing is a clue.

Which side is the driver's side Volkswagen Tiguan wing mirror in Ireland?

The right-hand (offside) mirror. The left-hand (nearside) mirror is the passenger side.
