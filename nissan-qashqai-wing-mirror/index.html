Nissan Qashqai wing mirror: generations, types and replacement

Which Nissan Qashqai mirror you need, how generations differ and what to check before you order.

Nissan Qashqai generations and mirror differences

GenerationYearsMirror notes
J102007-2013The first Qashqai. A Qashqai+2 version also exists. Electric heated mirrors are common.
J112013-2021Second generation with a different mirror. Some trims have extra equipment, so check yours.
J122021 onwardsCurrent generation with a new design. Not interchangeable with earlier cars.

Year ranges are approximate and based on European sales. Check your registration and handbook for your car.

Which Nissan Qashqai mirror do you have?

Features depend on year, market and trim. Cars like yours may come with:

  • Electric adjustment on most cars
  • Heated glass on many trims
  • Folding on some trims
  • Indicator repeater on many cars
  • Camera or sensor equipment on some higher trims of newer cars

Test your own car: does the mirror adjust electrically, fold, show an indicator, or clear mist with the rear screen heater on? Match those features when ordering.

Common Nissan Qashqai wing mirror problems

  • Cracked or detached glass
  • Fold motor not working on older cars
  • Damage from gate posts and tight car parks

Tip for your Qashqai

Always say which generation you have. J10, J11 and J12 are different parts, and the right pick depends on year, side and features.

Glass, cap or full unit?

If only the glass is cracked, glass alone is usually enough. A broken housing, a dead motor or a failed indicator usually means a full unit. See glass vs full unit and replacing a wing mirror.

Nissan Qashqai wing mirror FAQs

Does my Qashqai mirror have a camera?

Some higher trims of newer cars have camera equipment. If you see a small lens under the mirror, you need a mirror that includes it.

What is the difference between J10 and J11 mirrors?

They belong to different generations, so they differ in shape and fittings. Do not assume they swap.

Which side is the driver's side Nissan Qashqai wing mirror in Ireland?

The right-hand (offside) mirror. The left-hand (nearside) mirror is the passenger side.
