Which BMW 3 Series mirror you need, how generations differ and what to check before you order.

Which BMW 3 Series mirror do you have?

Features depend on year, market and trim. Cars like yours may come with:

  • Electric adjustment and heated glass on most cars
  • Power folding on many trims
  • Auto-dimming glass on some trims
  • Memory function on some higher trims
  • Blind spot detection on some cars
  • Indicator repeater in the housing

Test your own car: does the mirror adjust electrically, fold, show an indicator, or clear mist with the rear screen heater on? Match those features when ordering.

Common BMW 3 Series wing mirror problems

  • Glass detaching or clips breaking
  • Fold motor faults
  • Heating failure

Tip for your 3 Series

Memory mirrors link to the driver's seat on some cars. Replace like for like to keep this working.

Glass, cap or full unit?

If only the glass is cracked, glass alone is usually enough. A broken housing, a dead motor or a failed indicator usually means a full unit. See glass vs full unit and replacing a wing mirror.

BMW 3 Series wing mirror FAQs

Does the 3 Series Touring use the same mirror as the saloon?

Often similar within a generation, but confirm with your options.

Does an F30 mirror fit a G20?

No. They are different generations.

Which side is the driver's side BMW 3 Series wing mirror in Ireland?

The right-hand (offside) mirror. The left-hand (nearside) mirror is the passenger side.
