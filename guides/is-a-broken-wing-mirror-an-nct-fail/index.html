Is a broken wing mirror an NCT fail?

The NCT includes checks on a car's mirrors. A mirror that is missing, loose, badly cracked or not giving a clear view can cause a problem. The exact outcome depends on the tester's assessment and the extent of the damage, so treat the information below as general guidance.

Things testers may look at

  • Is the mirror present and securely fixed?
  • Is the glass intact and giving a usable view?
  • Is the housing damaged enough to be unsafe?

What to do before the test

  1. Replace shattered glass, which is usually quick and cheap.
  2. Tighten or refit loose housings.
  3. If the housing is broken, replace the full unit.

For current requirements, check the NCT test standards on ncts.ie. See also replacing a wing mirror.
