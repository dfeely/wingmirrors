Wing mirror glass vs full unit

Choose glass only when

  • The glass is cracked, scratched or has fallen out.
  • The housing, motor and indicator work and look intact.

Choose a full unit when

  • The housing is smashed or the mount is damaged.
  • The motor no longer adjusts or folds.
  • The indicator or wiring has failed.
  • The mirror hangs from its wires.

What about caps?

If only the coloured cover is scuffed or broken, a replacement cap may be all you need.
