Wing mirror replacement cost in Ireland

There is no single price. The cost depends on what you replace and how complex the mirror is. Here are the factors, not fixed prices, because they vary by supplier, car and time.

What changes the price

  • Part type: glass is the cheapest, caps are next, complete units cost the most.
  • Features: heating, folding, indicators, blind spot sensors and auto-dimming add cost.
  • Brand and age: premium makes and newer models usually cost more.
  • New vs used vs aftermarket: used and aftermarket parts can be cheaper, but check fit and quality.
  • Fitting: doing the job yourself saves labour, and glass is an easy DIY job on many cars.

Ways to save

  • Check whether glass only will solve the issue.
  • Compare new, used and aftermarket options.
  • Get your insurer's view if the damage was from a collision.
