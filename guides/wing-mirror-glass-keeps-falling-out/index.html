Wing mirror glass keeps falling out: causes and fixes

Safety first: wear gloves and eye protection around cracked glass, and check your vehicle handbook before taking anything apart. If your car has airbags, sensors or cameras near the door and you are not confident, use a qualified fitter.

Glass that pops out, rattles or hangs loose is one of the most common wing mirror problems. It is usually a quick fix once you know what failed.

Common causes

  • Broken retaining clips: the glass is held onto a backing plate by plastic clips, and they can snap with age or after a knock.
  • Cracked backing plate: if the plate behind the glass is broken, the glass has nothing solid to clip to.
  • A failed adhesive pad: some mirrors use a bonded pad that has let go.
  • Vibration and age: older mirrors can wear until the glass no longer sits tight.
  • A heavy knock: a car park bump or a pressure wash used too close can dislodge glass.

What to check first

  1. Look at the back of the glass for broken clip posts or snapped lugs.
  2. Check the backing plate on the mirror. If it is cracked or missing pieces, glass alone will not hold.
  3. If the glass is heated, note whether the heater wires are still attached.

How to fix it

Backing plate intact

Fit new glass for the correct side. It should clip on firmly. Our step-by-step guide explains how.

Backing plate broken

You will usually need the full mirror unit, or at least a replacement glass and plate set where available. See glass vs full unit.

Temporary fixes

Tape or adhesive can hold glass for a short time, but they are not a reliable repair. A loose mirror can give a poor view while driving, so replace it as soon as you can.

Frequently asked questions

Why does my wing mirror glass fall out in cold weather?

Cold makes plastic clips more brittle, and ice can add pressure. If the glass keeps coming out, the clips or backing plate are probably worn.

Can I glue wing mirror glass back in?

A temporary glue repair may hold for a while, but it can make future replacement harder and may not give a safe, stable view. Replacing the glass or the unit is the proper fix.
