Advice for choosing and replacing wing mirrors on Mercedes-Benz cars sold in Ireland.

About Mercedes-Benz wing mirrors

From the A-Class to the Sprinter van, Mercedes mirrors cover a wide range. Higher trims have power folding, auto-dimming, blind spot assist and sometimes camera or sensor equipment in the housing. Commercial Vito and Sprinter mirrors also differ between standard and extended arms.

What to check before ordering

  • Which side: driver's (right) or passenger (left).
  • Glass only, cap only or complete unit.
  • Electric or manual adjustment, and whether it is heated.
  • Folding, indicator, blind spot or auto-dimming features.
  • Year of manufacture, since parts change at facelifts.

Mercedes-Benz wing mirror FAQs

Are Sprinter mirrors the same as car mirrors?

No. Vans use bigger housings and different mounting, often with separate convex sections.

What if my Mercedes has blind spot assist?

You will need a mirror that includes the sensor function. Fitting a basic unit may disable the warning.

Which side is the driver's side Mercedes-Benz wing mirror in Ireland?

The right-hand (offside) mirror. The left-hand (nearside) mirror is the passenger side.
