Kia Ceed wing mirror: generations, types and replacement

Which Kia Ceed mirror you need, how generations differ and what to check before you order.

Kia Ceed generations and mirror differences

GenerationYearsMirror notes
First generation2007-2012Often written cee'd. Simple mirrors on many cars.
Second generation2012-2018Updated styling. Electric heated mirrors common.
Third generation2018 onwardsNewer design with more driver-assist options.

Year ranges are approximate and based on European sales. Check your registration and handbook for your car.

Which Kia Ceed mirror do you have?

Features depend on year, market and trim. Cars like yours may come with:

  • Manual or electric adjustment depending on year
  • Heated glass on many trims
  • Indicator repeater on many cars
  • Folding on some trims
  • Blind spot warning on some newer trims

Test your own car: does the mirror adjust electrically, fold, show an indicator, or clear mist with the rear screen heater on? Match those features when ordering.

Common Kia Ceed wing mirror problems

  • Glass working loose
  • Cracked housing
  • Indicator faults

Tip for your Ceed

Older cars are often written cee'd, so search both spellings when comparing parts.

Glass, cap or full unit?

If only the glass is cracked, glass alone is usually enough. A broken housing, a dead motor or a failed indicator usually means a full unit. See glass vs full unit and replacing a wing mirror. For all your options, see wing mirror replacement.

Kia Ceed wing mirror FAQs

Is the cee'd mirror the same as the Ceed?

They are generations of the same model line, but different parts across generations. Match your year.

Can I replace the glass on my Ceed?

Yes, if the housing is fine. Check the side and whether the glass is heated.

Which side is the driver's side Kia Ceed wing mirror in Ireland?

The right-hand (offside) mirror. The left-hand (nearside) mirror is the passenger side.

Need help finding the right mirror?

Send us your registration number, which side (driver or passenger) and a photo of the back of the mirror, and we will point you to the correct part.
