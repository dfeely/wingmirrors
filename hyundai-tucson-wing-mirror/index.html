Hyundai Tucson wing mirror: generations, types and replacement

Which Hyundai Tucson mirror you need, how generations differ and what to check before you order.

Hyundai Tucson generations and mirror differences

GenerationYearsMirror notes
Tucson (JM)2004-2010The first Tucson, with simple mirrors on many cars.
ix35 (LM)2010-2015Sold as the ix35 in Europe, so look for ix35 mirrors.
Tucson (TL)2015-2020The Tucson name returned. Some trims have blind spot detection.
Tucson (NX4)2020 onwardsNew design with a different mirror and more equipment.

Year ranges are approximate and based on European sales. Check your registration and handbook for your car.

Which Hyundai Tucson mirror do you have?

Features depend on year, market and trim. Cars like yours may come with:

  • Electric adjustment on most cars
  • Heated glass on many trims
  • Folding on some trims
  • Indicator repeater in the mirror on many cars
  • Blind spot detection on some trims, with a light in the mirror

Test your own car: does the mirror adjust electrically, fold, show an indicator, or clear mist with the rear screen heater on? Match those features when ordering.

Common Hyundai Tucson wing mirror problems

  • Indicator faults on built-in repeaters
  • Cracked glass
  • Housing damage from parking knocks

Tip for your Tucson

If your car is a 2010-2015 model, check whether it is badged ix35, as parts catalogues often list it separately.

Glass, cap or full unit?

If only the glass is cracked, glass alone is usually enough. A broken housing, a dead motor or a failed indicator usually means a full unit. See glass vs full unit and replacing a wing mirror.

Hyundai Tucson wing mirror FAQs

Is the ix35 mirror the same as a Tucson mirror?

They are different generations, so the parts differ. Check which badge and year your car has.

Does my Tucson have blind spot detection?

Some trims do. If there is a warning light in the mirror, order a mirror that supports it.

Which side is the driver's side Hyundai Tucson wing mirror in Ireland?

The right-hand (offside) mirror. The left-hand (nearside) mirror is the passenger side.
