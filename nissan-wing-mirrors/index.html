Nissan wing mirrors

Advice for choosing and replacing wing mirrors on Nissan cars sold in Ireland.

About Nissan wing mirrors

The Qashqai is one of Ireland's best-selling used crossovers, and the Juke and Micra also feature strongly. Many Nissans combine heated glass, electric adjustment and a repeater indicator, so the correct plug type is key. The Leaf is a different case, with some versions using a different mirror specification again.

What to check before ordering

  • Which side: driver's (right) or passenger (left).
  • Glass only, cap only or complete unit.
  • Electric or manual adjustment, and whether it is heated.
  • Folding, indicator, blind spot or auto-dimming features.
  • Year of manufacture, since parts change at facelifts.

Nissan wing mirror FAQs

How do I know which Qashqai mirror I need?

Note the generation (J10 or J11), the side, and whether yours has heating, folding and an indicator.

Are Nissan Leaf mirrors different?

They can be. Electric models sometimes have slightly different fittings or aerodynamic shapes, so match the exact model and year.

Which side is the driver's side Nissan wing mirror in Ireland?

The right-hand (offside) mirror. The left-hand (nearside) mirror is the passenger side.

Need help finding the right mirror?

Send us your registration number, which side (driver or passenger) and a photo of the back of the mirror, and we will point you to the correct part.
