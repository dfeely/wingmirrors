Advice for choosing and replacing wing mirrors on Citroën cars sold in Ireland.

About Citroën wing mirrors

Citroën's C3, C4 and Berlingo are widely used in Ireland, and the C1 shares a platform with the Toyota Aygo and Peugeot 107 though the mirror parts are branded differently. Many Berlingo owners are tradespeople who need a quick, affordable fix to stay on the road.

What to check before ordering

  • Which side: driver's (right) or passenger (left).
  • Glass only, cap only or complete unit.
  • Electric or manual adjustment, and whether it is heated.
  • Folding, indicator, blind spot or auto-dimming features.
  • Year of manufacture, since parts change at facelifts.

Citroën wing mirror FAQs

Does the Citroën C1 use the same mirror as the Aygo?

Do not assume so. Parts can look similar but differ in fit and fittings. Match the exact car.

What mirror options does a Berlingo have?

Typically manual or electric, with or without heating. Check yours first.

Which side is the driver's side Citroën wing mirror in Ireland?

The right-hand (offside) mirror. The left-hand (nearside) mirror is the passenger side.

Need help finding the right mirror?

Send us your registration number, which side (driver or passenger) and a photo of the back of the mirror, and we will point you to the correct part.
