Toyota Yaris wing mirror: generations, types and replacement

Which Toyota Yaris mirror you need, how generations differ and what to check before you order.

Toyota Yaris generations and mirror differences

GenerationYearsMirror notes
XP902005-2011Earlier cars. Some versions had electric adjustment and a few had power folding. Check for a mirror switch and a fold button.
XP1302011-2020The most common Yaris here. The 2014 and 2017 facelifts changed styling, so the mirror shape and options vary within this generation. Hybrid versions joined the range in this period.
XP2102020 onwardsNewer generation with a different mirror design. Not interchangeable with the XP130. The separate Yaris Cross is a different car again.

Year ranges are approximate and based on European sales. Check your registration and handbook for your car.

Which Toyota Yaris mirror do you have?

Features depend on year, market and trim. Cars like yours may come with:

  • Manual adjustment on base trims of older cars
  • Electric adjustment on many mid and upper trims
  • Heated glass on some trims, often tied to the rear screen heater
  • Power folding on some versions
  • Indicator repeater in the housing on many later cars

Test your own car: does the mirror adjust electrically, fold, show an indicator, or clear mist with the rear screen heater on? Match those features when ordering.

Common Toyota Yaris wing mirror problems

  • Cracked or scraped mirror caps from tight parking
  • Glass working loose from the backing plate
  • Indicator or fold function stopping on cars with built-in electrics

Tip for your Yaris

Look at the facelift year first. A 2012 and a 2018 Yaris can look similar from the driver's seat but use different mirrors.

Glass, cap or full unit?

If only the glass is cracked, glass alone is usually enough. A broken housing, a dead motor or a failed indicator usually means a full unit. See glass vs full unit and replacing a wing mirror. For all your options, see wing mirror replacement.

Toyota Yaris wing mirror FAQs

Does a Yaris wing mirror fit across 2011-2020 cars?

Not always. The 2014 and 2017 updates changed styling and options, so confirm the year and whether yours is manual or electric before ordering.

Is the Yaris hybrid mirror different?

The hybrid shares the body with the standard car, but the trim and options can differ. Match your mirror's features, not just the model name.

Which side is the driver's side Toyota Yaris wing mirror in Ireland?

The right-hand (offside) mirror. The left-hand (nearside) mirror is the passenger side.
