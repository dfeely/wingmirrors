Ford Transit wing mirror: generations, types and replacement

Which Ford Transit mirror you need, how generations differ and what to check before you order.

Ford Transit generations and mirror differences

GenerationYearsMirror notes
Older Transit (Mk6 / Mk7)2000-2013Older vans with larger, simpler mirrors. Manual or electric adjustment depending on trim.

Other Transit vansVariousThe Transit Custom and Transit Connect are different vans with different mirrors.

Year ranges are approximate and based on European sales. Check your registration and handbook for your car.

Which Ford Transit mirror do you have?

Features depend on year, market and trim. Cars like yours may come with:

  • Manual or electric adjustment
  • Heated glass on many vans
  • Wide-angle or convex lower glass on some
  • Indicator repeater on some versions
  • Extension or towing mirror arms
  • Power folding on some trims

Test your own car: does the mirror adjust electrically, fold, show an indicator, or clear mist with the rear screen heater on? Match those features when ordering.

Common Ford Transit wing mirror problems

  • Mirror knocked in tight sites and gateways
  • Loose arms or fixings from vibration
  • Cracked glass
  • Damaged lower convex glass

Tip for your Transit

Tell us if your Transit has a lower wide-angle section. The spotter glass is part of many vans' mirrors.

Glass, cap or full unit?

If only the glass is cracked, glass alone is usually enough. A broken housing, a dead motor or a failed indicator usually means a full unit. See glass vs full unit and replacing a wing mirror.

Ford Transit wing mirror FAQs

Is a Transit Custom mirror the same as a Transit?

No. They are different vans with different mirrors. Order for your exact model.

Can I replace the lower wide-angle glass only?

Sometimes. If the main housing is fine, a separate glass may be available for your model.

Which side is the driver's side Ford Transit wing mirror in Ireland?

The right-hand (offside) mirror. The left-hand (nearside) mirror is the passenger side.

Need help finding the right mirror?

Send us your registration number, which side (driver or passenger) and a photo of the back of the mirror, and we will point you to the correct part.
