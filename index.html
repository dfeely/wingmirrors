Why choose the right wing mirror first time?

A wing mirror can look simple, but the same model can come with very different parts: manual or electric adjustment, heated or unheated glass, folding motor, indicator repeater, blind spot warning and auto-dimming glass. Ordering the wrong version means wasted time and money, which is why we start with the questions that matter.

  • Which side is damaged: driver or passenger?
  • Is it the glass only, or the whole mirror unit?
  • Does yours have electric adjust, heating, folding or an indicator?
  • What is the year of your car and the trim level?

Frequently asked questions

What is a wing mirror?

A wing mirror is the exterior mirror mounted on the door or A-pillar of a car, van or truck. It lets the driver see traffic alongside and behind.

Which side is the driver's side wing mirror in Ireland?

Ireland drives on the left with right-hand-drive cars, so the driver's side is the right-hand (offside) mirror and the passenger side is the left-hand (nearside) mirror.

Can I replace just the mirror glass?

Yes, if the housing and motor are undamaged. Glass-only replacement is usually the cheapest repair.

Need help finding the right mirror?
