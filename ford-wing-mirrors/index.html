Ford wing mirrors

Advice for choosing and replacing wing mirrors on Ford cars sold in Ireland.

About Ford wing mirrors

Ford models such as the Fiesta, Focus and Transit are everywhere in Ireland, from family cars to vans. Van mirrors often have a separate wide-angle or lower convex glass, and commercial users should check this before ordering. Focus and Fiesta mirrors vary between facelift versions, so the build year matters.

What to check before ordering

  • Which side: driver's (right) or passenger (left).
  • Glass only, cap only or complete unit.
  • Electric or manual adjustment, and whether it is heated.
  • Folding, indicator, blind spot or auto-dimming features.
  • Year of manufacture, since parts change at facelifts.

Ford wing mirror FAQs

Are Transit wing mirrors different from car mirrors?

Yes. Vans often have larger housings, extra convex glass sections and sometimes extension arms. Check your exact model and year.

Does a Ford Focus mirror fit every year?

No. Shape and plug type change between generations and facelifts, so match your year of manufacture.

Which side is the driver's side Ford wing mirror in Ireland?

The right-hand (offside) mirror. The left-hand (nearside) mirror is the passenger side.
