Skoda wing mirrors

Advice for choosing and replacing wing mirrors on Skoda cars sold in Ireland.

About Skoda wing mirrors

Skoda shares much of its engineering with Volkswagen, so mirror construction on an Octavia or Fabia will feel familiar, but the housings and glass are brand specific. The Octavia is very popular in Ireland, particularly in diesel estate form, so expect a lot of used-car owners looking for replacements.

What to check before ordering

  • Which side: driver's (right) or passenger (left).
  • Glass only, cap only or complete unit.
  • Electric or manual adjustment, and whether it is heated.
  • Folding, indicator, blind spot or auto-dimming features.
  • Year of manufacture, since parts change at facelifts.

Skoda wing mirror FAQs

Can I use a VW Golf mirror on my Skoda Octavia?

Do not assume so. Platforms are related but mounting points, glass shape and caps differ. Order for your exact Skoda model and year.

What is the difference between mirror glass and a mirror unit on a Skoda?

The glass is the reflective plate that clips into the housing. The unit includes the housing, motor, wiring and often the indicator.

Which side is the driver's side Skoda wing mirror in Ireland?

The right-hand (offside) mirror. The left-hand (nearside) mirror is the passenger side.
