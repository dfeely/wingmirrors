Dacia Duster wing mirror: generations, types and replacement

Which Dacia Duster mirror you need, how generations differ and what to check before you order.

Dacia Duster generations and mirror differences

GenerationYearsMirror notes
First generation2010-2017Early Dusters, with facelifts during the period. Simpler mirrors on many cars.
Second generation2018-2024A more modern design. Mirror options increase on higher trims.
Third generation2024 onwardsLatest design with a new mirror shape. Not interchangeable with earlier cars.

Year ranges are approximate and based on European sales. Check your registration and handbook for your car.

Which Dacia Duster mirror do you have?

Features depend on year, market and trim. Cars like yours may come with:

  • Manual or electric adjustment depending on year and trim
  • Heated glass on some trims
  • Indicator repeater on some versions
  • Body-coloured or black caps
  • Blind spot warning on some newer trims

Test your own car: does the mirror adjust electrically, fold, show an indicator, or clear mist with the rear screen heater on? Match those features when ordering.

Common Dacia Duster wing mirror problems

  • Damage from gate posts and tight roads
  • Cracked glass
  • Cap scuffs from off-road or rural use

Tip for your Duster

Duster mirrors can look similar across generations, but they are not interchangeable. Check the year first.

Glass, cap or full unit?

If only the glass is cracked, glass alone is usually enough. A broken housing, a dead motor or a failed indicator usually means a full unit. See glass vs full unit and replacing a wing mirror. For all your options, see wing mirror replacement.

Dacia Duster wing mirror FAQs

Will a first-generation Duster mirror fit a second-generation car?

No. They are different generations with different housings and fittings.

Do all Dusters have electric mirrors?

No. Base trims often have manual adjustment. Check yours before ordering.

Which side is the driver's side Dacia Duster wing mirror in Ireland?

The right-hand (offside) mirror. The left-hand (nearside) mirror is the passenger side.
