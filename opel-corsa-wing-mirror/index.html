Opel Corsa wing mirror: generations, types and replacement

Which Opel Corsa mirror you need, how generations differ and what to check before you order.

Opel Corsa generations and mirror differences

GenerationYearsMirror notes
Corsa D2006-2014A facelift around 2010 sits within this range.
Corsa E2014-2019New design. Manual or electric depending on trim.
Corsa F2019 onwardsLatest generation, including an electric version.

Year ranges are approximate and based on European sales. Check your registration and handbook for your car.

Which Opel Corsa mirror do you have?

Features depend on year, market and trim. Cars like yours may come with:

  • Manual or electric adjustment depending on trim
  • Heated glass on some trims
  • Indicator repeater on some versions
  • Folding on some trims
  • Body-coloured or black caps

Test your own car: does the mirror adjust electrically, fold, show an indicator, or clear mist with the rear screen heater on? Match those features when ordering.

Common Opel Corsa wing mirror problems

  • Cracked glass
  • Cap damage
  • Adjustment faults on older cars

Tip for your Corsa

Corsas are sold in other markets as Vauxhalls, and parts catalogues may list them under either name.

Glass, cap or full unit?

If only the glass is cracked, glass alone is usually enough. A broken housing, a dead motor or a failed indicator usually means a full unit. See glass vs full unit and replacing a wing mirror. For all your options, see wing mirror replacement.

Opel Corsa wing mirror FAQs

Are Vauxhall and Opel Corsa mirrors the same?

Often yes for right-hand drive cars, but confirm the generation and year.

Does a Corsa D mirror fit a Corsa E?

No. They are different generations.

Which side is the driver's side Opel Corsa wing mirror in Ireland?

The right-hand (offside) mirror. The left-hand (nearside) mirror is the passenger side.
