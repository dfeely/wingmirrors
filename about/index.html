About wingmirrors.ie

wingmirrors.ie helps Irish drivers work out which wing mirror they need, how to replace it and what to check before they order. It is run by Feely Consultants, based in Dublin 16.

What you will find here

  • Guides for 26 car brands and 54 popular models, covering generations, mirror types and common faults.
  • 12 guides on replacing, choosing and troubleshooting wing mirrors, including wing mirror replacement, how to replace a wing mirror and problem-solving guides.
  • Part-type pages on mirror glass, covers and caps, towing mirrors and used mirrors.

How we write our guides

We base generation and year ranges on published manufacturer and industry information, and keep trim details general where specifications vary by market and trim. We aim to explain things plainly, tell you what to check on your own car, and say when a job is better left to a fitter.

Guides are general advice, not a substitute for your vehicle handbook. Where rules apply, such as NCT standards or towing requirements, we point you to the official source.

Independent of car makers

wingmirrors.ie is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, any car manufacturer. Brand and model names are used only to say which cars a guide covers.

Spotted a mistake, or want your car covered?

We want the guides to be accurate. If something is wrong or out of date, or you would like us to cover another model, please get in touch. Guides last reviewed: November 2026.
