Nissan Juke wing mirror: generations, types and replacement

Which Nissan Juke mirror you need, how generations differ and what to check before you order.

Nissan Juke generations and mirror differences

GenerationYearsMirror notes
F152010-2019The original Juke, with a facelift around 2014. Colour-coded caps are common.
F162019 onwardsNew design with a different mirror. Not interchangeable with the first generation.

Year ranges are approximate and based on European sales. Check your registration and handbook for your car.

Which Nissan Juke mirror do you have?

Features depend on year, market and trim. Cars like yours may come with:

  • Electric adjustment on most cars
  • Heated glass on many trims
  • Folding on some trims
  • Indicator repeater on many cars
  • Coloured or contrasting caps on some trims

Test your own car: does the mirror adjust electrically, fold, show an indicator, or clear mist with the rear screen heater on? Match those features when ordering.

Common Nissan Juke wing mirror problems

  • Cap damage
  • Cracked glass
  • Fold motor faults

Tip for your Juke

Juke caps are often a contrasting colour. If only the cap is broken, a cap on its own can be cheaper.

Glass, cap or full unit?

If only the glass is cracked, glass alone is usually enough. A broken housing, a dead motor or a failed indicator usually means a full unit. See glass vs full unit and replacing a wing mirror.

Nissan Juke wing mirror FAQs

Does a first-generation Juke mirror fit a second?

No. They are different designs.

Can I change just the cap on my Juke mirror?

Often yes. Match the side, colour and generation.

Which side is the driver's side Nissan Juke wing mirror in Ireland?

The right-hand (offside) mirror. The left-hand (nearside) mirror is the passenger side.
