SEAT wing mirrors

Advice for choosing and replacing wing mirrors on SEAT cars sold in Ireland.

About SEAT wing mirrors

SEAT shares a lot with Volkswagen and Skoda, but the mirrors are brand specific. The Ibiza and Leon are the most requested, with several body styles and facelifts, so confirm yours before ordering.

Popular SEAT models

  • Ibiza
  • Leon
  • Ateca
  • Arona
  • Alhambra

Tell us your exact model, year and trim and we can help you find the matching part.

What to check before ordering

  • Which side: driver's (right) or passenger (left).
  • Glass only, cap only or complete unit.
  • Electric or manual adjustment, and whether it is heated.
  • Folding, indicator, blind spot or auto-dimming features.
  • Year of manufacture, since parts change at facelifts.

SEAT wing mirror FAQs

Is a SEAT Leon mirror the same as a Golf mirror?

Not necessarily. Shared platform does not guarantee shared mirror parts.

What if my SEAT has painted mirror caps?

You can usually order caps in your car's colour code.

Which side is the driver's side SEAT wing mirror in Ireland?

The right-hand (offside) mirror. The left-hand (nearside) mirror is the passenger side.

Need help finding the right mirror?

Send us your registration number, which side (driver or passenger) and a photo of the back of the mirror, and we will point you to the correct part.
