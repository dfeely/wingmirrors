Which Skoda Octavia mirror you need, how generations differ and what to check before you order.

Which Skoda Octavia mirror do you have?

Features depend on year, market and trim. Cars like yours may come with:

  • Manual or electric adjustment depending on year and trim
  • Heated glass on many cars
  • Power folding on some trims
  • Blind spot warning on some cars, with a light in the mirror
  • Indicator repeater on some versions

Test your own car: does the mirror adjust electrically, fold, show an indicator, or clear mist with the rear screen heater on? Match those features when ordering.

Common Skoda Octavia wing mirror problems

  • Glass cracked or detached
  • Heated mirror elements stopping working
  • Adjustment motor failure on older cars

Tip for your Octavia

Estate and hatchback Octavias of the same generation share mirror designs, but always confirm your year and options.

Glass, cap or full unit?

If only the glass is cracked, glass alone is usually enough. A broken housing, a dead motor or a failed indicator usually means a full unit. See glass vs full unit and replacing a wing mirror. For all your options, see wing mirror replacement.

Skoda Octavia wing mirror FAQs

Is an Octavia mirror the same as a Golf mirror?

No. The cars are related but the mirrors are different parts. Order for your Octavia's generation.

My Octavia mirror won't clear ice, is the glass faulty?

Possibly. The heater element in the glass can fail, and replacing the glass is often the fix. Check the connections first.

Which side is the driver's side Skoda Octavia wing mirror in Ireland?

The right-hand (offside) mirror. The left-hand (nearside) mirror is the passenger side.
