Wing mirror replacement in Ireland: your options explained

Glass, cap or full unit? How to choose the right replacement, where to get it and who should fit it.

Whether your mirror is cracked, knocked off or just not working, this page walks you through wing mirror replacement from start to finish: what to replace, how to get the right part and who should fit it.

What needs replacing?

What is wrongWhat you needWho can fit it
Cracked or loose glass, housing and motor fineReplacement glassEasy DIY on most cars
Scuffed or broken outer coverReplacement capUsually DIY, may need painting
Broken housing, dead motor or failed indicatorComplete mirror unitDIY or a fitter

Step 1: identify the exact mirror

A replacement only works if it matches your car. Before you order, note:

  • Side: in Ireland the right-hand mirror is the driver's side and the left is the passenger side. See wing side mirror.
  • Make, model and year: generations and facelifts change the shape and plug.
  • Features: electric adjustment, heating, folding, indicator, blind spot warning, auto-dimming, puddle light or camera.

Step 3: decide who fits it

  • Do it yourself: glass and caps are the easiest jobs. Follow our step-by-step guide to replacing a wing mirror.
  • A fitter or garage: a sensible choice for full units on complex cars, or if your mirror has sensors, cameras or memory functions.
  • Through insurance: if someone damaged your car, see what to do when your mirror is damaged in a car park.

Frequently asked questions

How long does wing mirror replacement take?

Replacing glass usually takes 5 to 15 minutes. A complete mirror unit often takes 30 to 90 minutes, depending on the car and whether the door trim has to come off.

Can I replace just the glass instead of the whole wing mirror?

Yes, if the housing, motor and indicator are undamaged. Glass-only replacement is usually the cheapest repair, but you must match the side and whether the glass is heated.

Is a used or aftermarket wing mirror OK?

Often yes, if it matches your car's exact model, year, side and features. Check the condition, the plug and any extras such as heating, folding or blind spot warning before you buy.

Can I replace a wing mirror myself?

Many drivers can fit replacement glass or a cap themselves. A full unit is more involved, and cars with cameras, sensors or complex door electrics are better left to a fitter.

Do I need to paint a replacement wing mirror cap?

Many caps are supplied in primer and need painting to match your car. Some come in black or a colour finish, so check what you are buying.

Will a broken wing mirror fail the NCT?

It can. A missing, loose or badly damaged mirror may cause a problem. See our guide on whether a broken wing mirror is an NCT fail, and check the current NCT standards.
