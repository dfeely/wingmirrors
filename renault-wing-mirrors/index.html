Renault wing mirrors

Advice for choosing and replacing wing mirrors on Renault cars sold in Ireland.

About Renault wing mirrors

The Clio and Captur are popular across Ireland, and the Trafic and Master are common in vans. Mirror specs differ a lot between trims and facelifts, and some models use cable adjust on the base trim and electric on the higher ones. Check which one you have before buying.

What to check before ordering

  • Which side: driver's (right) or passenger (left).
  • Glass only, cap only or complete unit.
  • Electric or manual adjustment, and whether it is heated.
  • Folding, indicator, blind spot or auto-dimming features.
  • Year of manufacture, since parts change at facelifts.

Renault wing mirror FAQs

Is my Clio mirror manual or electric?

If there is a joystick or switch inside the door or dashboard, it is electric. If you adjust it by hand or with a small lever inside, it is manual.

Do Renault mirror plugs vary?

Yes. Heated and folding versions use different connectors, so match the exact trim.

Which side is the driver's side Renault wing mirror in Ireland?

The right-hand (offside) mirror. The left-hand (nearside) mirror is the passenger side.
