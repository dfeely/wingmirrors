Ford Fiesta wing mirror: generations, types and replacement

Which Ford Fiesta mirror you need, how generations differ and what to check before you order.

Ford Fiesta generations and mirror differences

GenerationYearsMirror notes
Mk62002-2008Older cars, often with basic mirrors.
Mk72008-2017The most common Fiesta here. A 2012 update changed the front end and some details. Heated aspheric glass is available for these cars.
Mk82017-2023Newer shape with more equipment. Not interchangeable with the Mk7.

Year ranges are approximate and based on European sales. Check your registration and handbook for your car.

Which Ford Fiesta mirror do you have?

Features depend on year, market and trim. Cars like yours may come with:

  • Manual adjustment on base trims
  • Electric adjustment and heated glass on many trims
  • Indicator repeater in the mirror on some cars
  • Power folding on some trims
  • Different glass shape on driver and passenger sides

Test your own car: does the mirror adjust electrically, fold, show an indicator, or clear mist with the rear screen heater on? Match those features when ordering.

Common Ford Fiesta wing mirror problems

  • Glass falling out or loose in the housing
  • Cracked mirror caps
  • Indicator faults on cars with a built-in repeater

Tip for your Fiesta

Fiesta glass is side-specific and may be heated or aspheric. Look at the back of the old glass to see whether it has heater terminals.

Glass, cap or full unit?

If only the glass is cracked, glass alone is usually enough. A broken housing, a dead motor or a failed indicator usually means a full unit. See glass vs full unit and replacing a wing mirror. For all your options, see wing mirror replacement.

Ford Fiesta wing mirror FAQs

Can I replace only the glass on my Fiesta?

Yes, if the housing and motor are fine. Check the side and whether the glass is heated.

Why does my Fiesta mirror look different from a friend's?

Facelifts and trim levels change the style and features. Match yours by year and specification.

Which side is the driver's side Ford Fiesta wing mirror in Ireland?

The right-hand (offside) mirror. The left-hand (nearside) mirror is the passenger side.
