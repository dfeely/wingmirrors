Nissan Micra wing mirror: generations, types and replacement

Which Nissan Micra mirror you need, how generations differ and what to check before you order.

Nissan Micra generations and mirror differences

GenerationYearsMirror notes
K122003-2010Older cars with simple mirrors.
K132010-2017Updated design. Manual or electric depending on trim.
K142017-2023Newer shape with more equipment on higher trims.

Year ranges are approximate and based on European sales. Check your registration and handbook for your car.

Which Nissan Micra mirror do you have?

Features depend on year, market and trim. Cars like yours may come with:

  • Manual adjustment on base trims
  • Electric adjustment on higher trims
  • Heated glass on some trims
  • Indicator repeater on some versions
  • Folding on some trims

Test your own car: does the mirror adjust electrically, fold, show an indicator, or clear mist with the rear screen heater on? Match those features when ordering.

Common Nissan Micra wing mirror problems

  • Cracked glass
  • Loose glass on older cars
  • Cap damage

Tip for your Micra

Micra owners often need only a glass or cap. Check what is actually broken before buying a full unit.

Glass, cap or full unit?

If only the glass is cracked, glass alone is usually enough. A broken housing, a dead motor or a failed indicator usually means a full unit. See glass vs full unit and replacing a wing mirror.

Nissan Micra wing mirror FAQs

Will a K12 Micra mirror fit a K13?

No. They are different generations.

Is the Micra mirror heated?

Some trims are. Check for heating terminals on the back of your glass.

Which side is the driver's side Nissan Micra wing mirror in Ireland?

The right-hand (offside) mirror. The left-hand (nearside) mirror is the passenger side.
