Dacia Jogger wing mirror: generations, types and replacement

Which Dacia Jogger mirror you need, how generations differ and what to check before you order.

Dacia Jogger generations and mirror differences

GenerationYearsMirror notes
Single generation2022 onwardsThe Jogger has been sold as one generation so far. Trim and equipment level determine which mirror you need.

Year ranges are approximate and based on European sales. Check your registration and handbook for your car.

Which Dacia Jogger mirror do you have?

Features depend on year, market and trim. Cars like yours may come with:

  • Electric adjustment on most cars
  • Heated glass on some trims
  • Indicator repeater on some versions
  • Body-coloured or contrasting caps depending on trim
  • Folding on some higher trims

Test your own car: does the mirror adjust electrically, fold, show an indicator, or clear mist with the rear screen heater on? Match those features when ordering.

Common Dacia Jogger wing mirror problems

  • Cracked glass or damaged caps from daily family use
  • Parking damage on a longer, wider car

Tip for your Jogger

Because the Jogger is a newer car, your car may still be under warranty. Check the terms before fitting any replacement part.

Glass, cap or full unit?

If only the glass is cracked, glass alone is usually enough. A broken housing, a dead motor or a failed indicator usually means a full unit. See glass vs full unit and replacing a wing mirror.

Dacia Jogger wing mirror FAQs

Is the Jogger mirror the same as the Sandero?

Do not assume so. Although they share a family resemblance, the parts differ. Order for your exact model.

Is my Jogger still under warranty?

If it is a recent car, possibly. Check with your dealer before fitting aftermarket parts.

Which side is the driver's side Dacia Jogger wing mirror in Ireland?

The right-hand (offside) mirror. The left-hand (nearside) mirror is the passenger side.

Need help finding the right mirror?

Send us your registration number, which side (driver or passenger) and a photo of the back of the mirror, and we will point you to the correct part.
