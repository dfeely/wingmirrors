Mazda wing mirrors

Advice for choosing and replacing wing mirrors on Mazda cars sold in Ireland.

About Mazda wing mirrors

Mazda owners tend to keep their cars a long time, so older mirrors with tired motors are common. The Mazda3 and CX-5 are the most sought-after. Heating, electric adjustment and indicator repeaters vary by trim and year.

What to check before ordering

  • Which side: driver's (right) or passenger (left).
  • Glass only, cap only or complete unit.
  • Electric or manual adjustment, and whether it is heated.
  • Folding, indicator, blind spot or auto-dimming features.
  • Year of manufacture, since parts change at facelifts.

Mazda wing mirror FAQs

Why does my Mazda mirror not adjust anymore?

The motor or switch may have failed, or the glass backing plate may have broken. Test the switch and listen for a motor sound first.

Is the MX-5 mirror the same as other Mazdas?

No. The MX-5 has its own body shape and mirror design.

Which side is the driver's side Mazda wing mirror in Ireland?

The right-hand (offside) mirror. The left-hand (nearside) mirror is the passenger side.

Need help finding the right mirror?

Send us your registration number, which side (driver or passenger) and a photo of the back of the mirror, and we will point you to the correct part.
