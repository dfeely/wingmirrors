Used wing mirrors in Ireland: how to buy a second-hand mirror

A used mirror can save you money, as long as it matches your car and works properly.

Is a used wing mirror a good idea?

Often yes. A used mirror from the same model and year is usually an original part, and it can cost much less than a new one. The risk is condition: you cannot always see damage, worn motors or faulty electrics before it arrives.

Where people look

  • Car dismantlers and breakers
  • Online classified and marketplace sites
  • Local garages and fitters who strip cars
  • Specialist parts sellers for your make

What to check before you buy

  1. Side: right is the driver's side in Ireland. See wing side mirror.
  2. Exact match: make, model, year and trim. Generations and facelifts change the shape.
  3. Features: electric adjust, heating, folding, indicator, blind spot warning, camera.
  4. The plug: ask for a photo and compare it with your old one.
  5. Condition: look at the glass, the cap, scratches and cracks around the housing.
  6. Function: ask whether the motor, fold and heating were tested.
  7. Colour: if the cap is painted, check the colour matches or can be repainted.
  8. Returns: ask what happens if it does not fit or does not work.

Frequently asked questions

Is a used wing mirror safe to fit?

Yes, if it matches your car, is undamaged and works properly. Check the glass, housing, motor and plug before fitting.

Can I fit a used mirror from a different year of the same model?

Often not. Generations and facelifts change the shape and plug. Match the exact year and features.

Should I buy a used mirror with cracked glass?

You can replace the glass, but check the backing plate and motor are fine. Otherwise it may not be worth it.
